## Release-boundary gates

- No unresolved architecture or contract clarification remains.
- External approvals and measurable baselines have a named owner/input, check point and fail-closed behavior; none is represented as an assumed value.
- Broader Course/RAG sources remain modeled but are not in the safe production MVP until their content-use right exists.
- No requirement depends on an LLM inventing a score, status, confidence value or learning mutation.
- `tasks.md` is the reviewed execution boundary; implementation status and command evidence are recorded in `implementation-evidence.md`.
- A complete task list and passing synthetic tests do not authorize production rollout while any named external gate remains closed.
